Delete your The Boyz account

This page explains how to permanently delete your The Boyz account and the personal data we hold about you. You can use it even if you have already uninstalled the app.

Option 1: delete from the app

  1. Open The Boyz and sign in.
  2. Go to Home → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password, then tap Delete permanently.

Your account is deleted straight away. You will be taken back to the sign-in screen.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in or have uninstalled the app, email us from the address you used to create your account:

Email: nishabt7311@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my The Boyz account

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm that you control the email address before we delete anything. We will confirm when the deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password).
  • Your display name and date of birth.
  • Your profile, including your bio, interests and what you are looking for.
  • Your profile photo.

What may be kept

Deletion requests are completed within 7 days. We do not keep your personal data after your account is deleted, except where the law requires us to keep certain records.

Backups

Deleted data is removed from our database backups within 7 days.
